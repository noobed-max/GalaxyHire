# GalaxyHire documentation

These pages describe the product from a user's point of view. They cover local setup, everyday workflows, AI provider settings, troubleshooting, and privacy. Internal design notes, handoffs, benchmarks, and historical project diaries are not part of the public documentation set.

## Choose a path

- [Getting started](getting-started.md) — install dependencies, start GalaxyHire, and load the extension.
- [User guide](user-guide.md) — search, profile management, document generation, applications, and tracking.
- [AI provider configuration](ai-providers.md) — connect OpenAI-compatible endpoints, Anthropic, OpenCode, local Ollama, or a subscription CLI.
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) — resolve startup, scraping, résumé parsing, provider, and extension issues.
- [Privacy and security](privacy-security.md) — understand local data, credentials, network calls, and safe usage.

## The product in one sentence

GalaxyHire helps you find roles, choose evidence from your own profile, generate application documents, and fill forms while leaving final decisions and submission to you.

## Safety reminder

Review every résumé, cover letter, and filled form. AI can make mistakes, job listings can be stale, and websites can change their form fields. GalaxyHire's extension is fill-only and does not press Submit.
